# Source research and adaptation boundaries

Accessed September 27, 2026. Public examples and guidance informed this original fictional packet. It is not a real transaction or FCT internal underwriting file. No source supplied the invented identities, amounts, address, authority threshold, dates, dispute, or lender conditions. Wording is original rather than copied form boilerplate. No institutional logos or signatures are reproduced.

1. **OnLand: public sample parcel register (actual sample PDF).**
   https://help.onland.ca/wp-content/uploads/English-Parcel-Register.pdf
   Informed BQ-03's separation of property identifier, ownership, description and instrument entries. No names or identifiers from the sample were reused. The dossier uses a readable narrative extract, not an official registry facsimile.

2. **OnLand: Parcel Register Options.**
   https://help.onland.ca/en/parcel-register-options/
   Describes selection of full history or a date range. Informed the restricted-date search in BQ-03. The missing earlier search is an invented case fact.

3. **Scotiabank: Ontario Standard Charge Terms, form 2153912 (07/18), sections 21, 22 and 28 (actual public PDF).**
   https://www.scotiabank.com/content/dam/scotiabank/canada/en/documents/mortgages/solicitor-forms/2153912_07_18_Final_Active.pdf
   These sections distinguish release/discharge mechanics and final reporting. They informed BQ-02 and BQ-06 as document types. The fictional lender's tenancy and funding conditions are author-created, not quoted bank policy. The dated source is not asserted to be the latest form for a current transaction.

4. **Financial Consumer Agency of Canada: Discharging a mortgage.**
   https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/mortgages/mortgage-discharge.html
   Explains that associated secured products can affect discharge and distinguishes paying a debt from the discharge process. Informed the two-facility conflict in BQ-06. Amounts and release conditions are invented.

5. **City of Toronto: Inspection Status Request, form 14-0038 (2013-11), actual public PDF.**
   https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/8e3f-14-0038-Inspection-Status-Request.pdf
   The form ties a request to a permit sequence and specified disciplines. Informed the mismatch between heating and coach-house records in BQ-08. This is a dated form example, not a claim about the current application channel.

6. **City of Toronto: Property Information Report.**
   https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/building-construction/building-permit/before-you-apply-for-a-building-permit/preliminary-zoning-reviews-information/property-information-reports/
   Describes reporting of permits, inspections and orders and says the report does not outline permitted use. Informed the limited scope of BQ-08. Toronto did not issue the fictional response.

7. **FCT: Search guidelines landing page.**
   https://fct.ca/search-guidelines
   Lists distinct residential and commercial resources by jurisdiction. Used as context only. This packet does not claim to apply FCT's actual search requirements, coverage rules or endorsements. A commercial or Quebec guide was not substituted for Ontario residential guidance.

## Research boundary

Public forms show document structure and issues worth asking about. They do not validate the resulting synthetic case or establish production reliability. No private customer file was obtained. The new case has not been reviewed by FCT or a licensed professional, and no live-model results are claimed.
